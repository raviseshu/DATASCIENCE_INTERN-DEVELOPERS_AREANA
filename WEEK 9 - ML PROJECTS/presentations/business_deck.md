# Slide 2: The Goal
* **Objective:** Automate property valuation.
* **Current State:** Manual estimates are slow and inconsistent.
* **Solution:** AI-driven price prediction based on historical data.

---

# Slide 3: What Drives Price?
* **Size Matters Most:** 69% of a home's value is determined by its Square Footage.
* **Location, Location, Location:** Being in a Rural vs. City area is the second biggest factor (20%).
* **Surprise:** Number of bathrooms has negligible impact compared to location.

---

# Slide 4: Model Performance
* **Baseline (Linear Regression):** 94% Accuracy.
* **Advanced (Random Forest):** 97% Accuracy.
* **Error Rate:** We are accurate to within ±5-6% of the true price on average.

---

# Slide 5: Business Impact
* **Efficiency:** Instant valuations for clients.
* **Consistency:** Removes human bias from initial estimates.
* **Scalability:** Can handle thousands of queries per minute.
