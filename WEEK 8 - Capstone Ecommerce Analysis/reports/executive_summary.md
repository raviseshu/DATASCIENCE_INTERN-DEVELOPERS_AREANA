**E-Commerce Sales Optimization & Customer Retention Analysis**

## 📊 BUSINESS OVERVIEW

This analysis examines 600 customer records to identify revenue drivers and churn predictors for a mid-sized e-commerce company. Our findings reveal clear pathways to increase revenue by 15% and reduce churn by 15% within 90 days.

---

## 🎯 KEY FINDINGS

### **Finding 1: Sales Predictability (R² = 98.1%)**
- **Insight:** Order quantity is the strongest sales driver
- **Statistic:** r = 0.9902 (p < 0.001)
- **Impact:** +1 unit increase → +$20,157 revenue
- **Recommendation:** Implement volume discount tiers (5+, 10+, 20+ units)

### **Finding 2: Churned Customers Pay More (38% Higher)**
- **Insight:** High-value customers paradoxically churn more
- **Statistic:** Churned: $86.23/mo vs Retained: $62.31/mo (t = -4.82, p < 0.001)
- **Impact:** Premium tier customers at risk
- **Recommendation:** Contract conversion incentive program

### **Finding 3: Tenure is Protective (21.7 Month Gap)**
- **Insight:** First 18 months are make-or-break for retention
- **Statistic:** Churned avg: 10.2 months, Retained avg: 31.9 months (t = 8.73, p < 0.001)
- **Impact:** Early investment pays $2M in LTV extension
- **Recommendation:** Heavy engagement during months 1-18

### **Finding 4: Contract Type Drives Churn (3.3× Difference)**
- **Insight:** Month-to-month contracts have 36% churn vs 5% for 2-year
- **Statistic:** χ² = 169.33 (p < 0.001)
- **Impact:** M2M contracts generate 3× churn risk
- **Recommendation:** Aggressive conversion to 1-year contracts
